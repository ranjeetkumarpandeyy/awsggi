About the event

Where Ludhiana's skill with looms meets the cloud.

Ludhiana has long been known as the textile and manufacturing centre of northern India. This event connects that tradition of skilled, practical work with present-day cloud computing.

The day has talks, hands-on labs and time to meet other students and working engineers. It is meant for students who want to learn how applications, data and AI services are built and run on AWS.
